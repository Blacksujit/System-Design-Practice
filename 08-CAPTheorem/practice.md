# CAP Theorem - Practice Problems

## Problem 1: Design a Distributed Database
**Requirements:**
- Global distribution
- Strong consistency for some operations
- High availability
- Handle network partitions

**Questions to Ask:**
- What consistency model?
- What replication factor?
- What conflict resolution?
